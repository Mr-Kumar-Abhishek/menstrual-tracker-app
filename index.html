Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy describes the Anonymous Menstrual Tracker app maintained in this repository and its project website. It reflects the app's current published source and verified offline architecture.

In brief

The app is designed to keep cycle and journal information strictly on your device. The current app source does not integrate advertising, analytics, or marketing SDKs, does not send your entries to the project maintainer, and does not provide an app account or cloud-sync service. We do not collect your health entries for marketing, sell them, or use them for targeted advertising.

Google Play, Android, GitHub, and other services you choose to use may process information independently under their own privacy policies. That platform processing is not data collected by the Anonymous Menstrual Tracker app or by its maintainer.

Information stored by the app

The app stores information you enter, such as cycle start and end dates, flow, symptoms, mood, notes, and preferences (including notification settings), in a local SQLite database. On first launch, the app may add sample cycle and journal entries when it finds no cycle history.

The app encrypts daily-log fields in the local database using authenticated AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is managed locally on-device. The app never transmits this information over the network or sends it to the maintainer.

Local notifications may show reminder text on your device. If you export information using an available export feature, the resulting file is ordinary JSON; protect and manage that file as you would other sensitive health information.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you remove them or clear the app's data. The maintainer cannot delete records remotely because the app does not send or provide access to them. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes local records; make an export first if you want to keep them.

Android builds allow platform backup according to your operating system settings. Depending on your device, Android version, account, and backup settings, app data may be included in device or cloud backups controlled by you and your platform provider. Removing data from the app may not remove existing platform backups. Check your Android and Google backup settings and the provider's privacy information.

Google Play, Android, and other providers

The app source does not include Google Analytics, Google advertising, Firebase, or another in-app marketing or analytics integration. We cannot state exactly what Google collects through Google Play, Android, Play Protect, or device backup; that may depend on how you obtain and use the app and on your settings. Those services are operated by Google under Google's own terms and privacy disclosures, not this app's data collection. See Google's Privacy Policy and the privacy information presented in Google Play.

The project website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and usage information as the website provider under its own policies. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. Following external links also takes you to services governed by their own policies.

Information you send to the maintainer

If you email the maintainer, they receive your email address and the message contents. They use that information to respond and manage the correspondence, not for marketing, and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose or to meet legal obligations. Do not include sensitive health details. You can ask about or request deletion of information from your message by contacting the address below.

GDPR and European privacy rights

The app processes entries locally to provide its cycle tracking features. The project maintainer does not receive those entries or maintain a user account or server-side copy. This policy is not a determination that a particular privacy law does or does not apply to a user's situation.

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, individuals may have rights such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent, as well as the right to complain to a supervisory authority. Because the maintainer has no access to local app records, requests concerning those records generally need to be handled on your device. You may contact the maintainer about this policy using the details below.

For information you voluntarily send to the maintainer, contact them to exercise any applicable rights. They may need to verify your request. For entries stored only on your device, the maintainer cannot access or change them on your behalf.

California privacy rights

California's Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), provides certain rights to California residents when it applies, including rights to know, access, correct, and delete covered personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. The app does not sell or share app entries for cross-context behavioral advertising, and the maintainer does not receive those entries. This policy does not waive any rights that apply to you.

Children and young people

The app has no account, age-verification, advertising, or social features. The maintainer does not knowingly collect or receive personal information from children through the app. The app is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian concerned about information stored on a child's device should manage or clear the app data on that device; the maintainer cannot access it remotely.

We aim to minimize data and avoid profiling or targeted advertising in line with child-privacy principles, including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and California's Age-Appropriate Design Code Act (CAADCA). This statement describes product practices; it is not a certification of compliance with COPPA, CAADCA, or any other law.

Security

Keeping entries on-device reduces information shared with the maintainer, but no storage or encryption method is perfectly secure. Protect your device with its screen lock, keep its software updated, and take care when creating or storing exports and backups.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated. Check the “Last updated” date above. Any new third-party services or data collection will be described here before making claims about them.
